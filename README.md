# 📊 Amazon Prime Video Content Analysis

## 📝 Project Overview
In the competitive world of video streaming platforms, **Amazon Prime Video** has emerged as a major player offering a vast library of shows and movies across multiple genres and regions. With continuous expansion in its content catalog, it becomes crucial to analyze viewer trends, content types, and platform growth over time.

This project leverages a rich dataset to uncover insights from Amazon Prime Video’s U.S. catalog, helping understand patterns that could guide **content creation**, **acquisition**, and **distribution strategies**.

---

## 📂 Dataset
The analysis is based on two CSV files:
- `titles.csv` — Includes metadata for over 9,000 unique titles.
- `credits.csv` — Contains more than 124,000 records of cast and crew information.

The dataset includes:
- Title name
- Genre
- Type (Movie/TV Show)
- Release year
- IMDb score and ratings
- Cast and crew roles (directors, actors, etc.)

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze the composition of Prime Video’s content.
- Visualize the growth in titles over time.
- Explore genre distributions and their popularity.
- Study trends in cast and crew contributions.
- Generate insights to support business and content decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📈 Key Insights (Examples)
- Most frequent genres and release year trends.
- Distribution of content types (TV Shows vs Movies).
- Top recurring actors/directors.
- IMDb score distribution across genres and years.
